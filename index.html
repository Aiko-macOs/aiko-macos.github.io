How It Works

1. Import Audio

Drag any audio or video file into Aiko—or record directly. Supports meetings, lectures, podcasts, and more.

2. Transcribe Offline

Aiko runs Whisper locally to generate a high-accuracy transcript. Processing completes in the background while you work.

3. Edit and Refine

Use word-replacement rules to fix recurring mistakes automatically. Preview and tweak as needed.

4. Export

Copy text, save as .srt subtitles, or export to your preferred format. Ready for notes, captions, or sharing.

FAQ

Is transcription really offline?

Yes. Powered by Whisper running entirely on your device—no internet or cloud required.

Does it support live transcription?

Aiko focuses on post-recording accuracy and does not offer real-time transcription.

Can it detect speakers?

Current version prioritizes core transcription quality; speaker detection is not included.

What file formats work?

Most common audio and video formats, including MP3, MP4, WAV, and more.

How accurate is it?

Exceptionally precise, favoring quality over speed for professional-grade results.
